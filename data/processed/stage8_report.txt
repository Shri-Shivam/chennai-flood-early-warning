1. METHODOLOGY
This backtest reuses Stage 7's already-trained, already-frozen LOEO
models and their already OOF-selected thresholds. Nothing is retrained
or re-tuned here. Stage 8's contribution is event-level granularity
(one result per individual flood timestamp) plus a pooled view across
all 5 known events, neither of which Stage 7 computed.

2. DATA SOURCES
- data/processed/inundation_risk_ml_dataset.csv (real, 353,130 rows;
  local-only per .gitignore, not committed to GitHub)
- data/processed/ai1_retrospective_forecast_2021.csv (real Stage 6 output,
  trained strictly through 2021-10-31 23:00)
- models/stage7_integrated/*.json (real, committed, frozen Stage 7 models)
- data/processed/stage7_model_comparison.csv (frozen thresholds, reused
  verbatim, never re-selected here)

3. LEAKAGE / TEMPORAL CONTROLS (inherited from Stage 6 and Stage 7)
- Every event is scored using the frozen model trained on the OTHER
  episode -- never the episode containing that event.
- AI#1 probability is merged on exact timestamp only; a missing
  required timestamp raises a hard error.
- Class 2 (uncertain) rows excluded from this binary evaluation.
- No threshold is selected in this script -- all thresholds are frozen
  values copied from stage7_model_comparison.csv.

4. EXPERIMENT DEFINITIONS
- Exp_A_Baseline: observed antecedent rainfall (rain_1h..rain_24h) +
  terrain (elevation, slope, distance_to_drainage). No forecast signal.
- Exp_B_Baseline_plus_AI1: Exp A features + AI#1's forecast probability
  (the only genuinely forward-looking / forecast-driven signal here).
- Exp_C_NonAI_Rain24h: deterministic, non-AI score = raw rain_24h (mm).
  Brier score is not applicable (NaN) for this experiment -- it is not
  a probability.

5. IMPORTANT MODELING DISTINCTION
Exp A's rainfall features are OBSERVED antecedent accumulations
available at each timestamp T, not a forecast. The only forecast-driven
component in this backtest is AI#1's probability in Exp B. This is a
retrospective integration backtest of already-fitted models, not an
independent real-time operational simulation.

8. BASELINE COMPARISON
Exp B's pooled CSI (0.0070) is LOWER than Exp A's (0.0077).
This pooled comparison combines two structurally different events (4 timestamps from Episode_1, 1 from Episode_2) and should be read alongside the event-level breakdown above, not in isolation -- a pooled number can hide event-to-event inconsistency.

9. SCIENTIFIC CONCLUSION
Only two independently verified flood episodes (5 total event timestamps) exist in this project's ground-truth data. These results are retrospective proof-of-concept evidence for the specific 5 historical moments evaluated, not evidence of general operational accuracy. Stage 7 already found no consistent incremental value from AI#1 integration across its two LOEO directions; this event-level view does not overturn that finding -- see the per-event breakdown above for where (if anywhere) Exp B and Exp A diverge.

10. LIMITATIONS
- Only 5 known flood event timestamps total; no statistical claim of
  generalization is made or intended.
- This backtest reuses Stage 7's frozen models; it does not repeat or
  re-verify Stage 7's own spatial-block inner-CV leakage controls --
  those were already established and tested in Stage 7 itself.
- Exp_C's Brier score is not applicable (raw mm is not a probability).
- '250m spatial risk' does not imply 250m rainfall resolution --
  rainfall features are a single time series broadcast across the
  spatial grid; spatial discrimination comes from terrain/drainage.
- No exact flood depth, exact flood arrival time, or exact number of
  affected people is claimed anywhere in this report.
