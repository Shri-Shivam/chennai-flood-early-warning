5. VALIDATION METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Outer validation: Leave-One-Episode-Out (both directions).
  Experiment A: train Episode_1, test Episode_2
  Experiment B: train Episode_2, test Episode_1
The held-out episode is not used for feature selection, model selection,
hyperparameter tuning, class weights, or threshold selection.
Random row splits and ordinary random K-fold are not used.

6. SPATIAL BLOCK METHODOLOGY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Block CRS: EPSG:32644
Centroids are stored as WGS84 lon/lat (EPSG:4326) and transformed to
EPSG:32644 (UTM zone 44N, metres) before blocking. Degree units are not
treated as kilometres.
Block size: 5000.0 m x 5000.0 m
block_ix = floor(easting / 5000), block_iy = floor(northing / 5000)
Inner folds split unique spatial_block_id values (Group/block K-fold).
Spatial buffer: 500.0 m
For each inner fold, training cells whose projected centroid is within
the buffer distance of any validation-fold cell are dropped from that
fold's training set. Validation cells are never dropped by the buffer.
Each cell therefore still receives exactly one out-of-fold score.

7. MODELS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
majority_baseline:
  Constant score = training-set positive prevalence.
  Always predicts class 0 (majority). Threshold is not CSI-optimized
  because a constant score makes CSI maximization degenerate
  (all-positive can yield CSI = prevalence > 0).
elevation_drainage_heuristic:
  Transparent, equal-weight score. NOT a hydraulic model.
  Training-only min-max normalization:
    elev_score  = (elev_max - elevation) / (elev_max - elev_min)
    drain_score = (drain_max - distance_to_drainage) / (drain_max - drain_min)
    score = 0.5 * elev_score + 0.5 * drain_score
    clipped to [0, 1] for probability-like metrics (Brier).
  Low elevation and shorter mapped OSM drainage distance increase the score.
  distance_to_drainage is distance to mapped OSM waterways, not hydraulic connectivity.
  Normalization limits come from the outer training episode (inner folds use
  inner-training limits for OOF scores). Slope is not used in this heuristic.
logistic_regression:
  StandardScaler fit on training features only.
  L2 logistic regression, C=1.0, solver=lbfgs.
  class_weight = balanced, computed from the training labels of that fit.
xgboost:
  Constrained search on inner spatial OOF PR-AUC:
    max_depth in {3,4,5}, n_estimators in {100,300},
    learning_rate=0.05, subsample=0.8, colsample_bytree=0.8, random_state=42
  scale_pos_weight = n_neg/n_pos from the training rows of that fit only.

8. THRESHOLD SELECTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Criterion: maximize CSI on out-of-fold scores from the outer training episode.
Ties: higher recall, then lower threshold.
The held-out episode is not used to choose the threshold.
Majority baseline is the documented exception (always class 0).
Selected thresholds:
  majority_baseline | train Episode_1 -> test Episode_2: threshold=1.000000
  elevation_drainage_heuristic | train Episode_1 -> test Episode_2: threshold=0.858758

  majority_baseline | train Episode_2 -> test Episode_1: threshold=1.000000
  elevation_drainage_heuristic | train Episode_2 -> test Episode_1: threshold=0.918873
  logistic_regression | train Episode_2 -> test Episode_1: threshold=0.827481
  xgboost | train Episode_2 -> test Episode_1: threshold=0.950000

9. METRICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary: PR-AUC (ranking), CSI at the selected threshold, recall at that threshold.
Secondary: precision, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix (TP/FP/FN/TN), Brier score.
Calibration (Brier) is exploratory because only two verified episodes exist.
CSI = TP / (TP + FP + FN)

11. LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Only two independently verified flood episodes are available.
- Results are exploratory event-generalization evidence, not operational skill.
- Labels are footprint presence/absence, not flood depth or arrival time.
- Rainfall is not cell-specific; spatial discrimination comes from terrain.
- distance_to_drainage is OSM waterway proximity, not hydraulic connectivity.
- Elevation is terrain elevation, not a flow-accumulation substitute.
- Class 2 exclusion reduces sample size and does not resolve label uncertainty.
- Inner spatial CV still uses cells from the same meteorological event.
- Episode_2 has only 40 class-1 cells; some inner spatial folds had 0
  validation positives because those inundated cells are clustered.
- CSI-selected thresholds can be high; a model may rank well (PR-AUC)
  and still issue zero alerts on the held-out episode (CSI = 0).
- No nationwide or universal flood-prediction claim is supported.

12. SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This stage asks whether observed rainfall plus terrain can rank inundation
risk across two historical Chennai flood episodes without spatial/event leakage.
It does not simulate hydraulics, predict depth, or forecast arrival time.
If ML models do not beat the majority or elevation/drainage baselines on
held-out episodes, that is a valid scientific result for this baseline.
Do not interpret a high TN count as flood-prediction accuracy: negatives dominate.
